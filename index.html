Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unauthorised Paradox ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you use or play the Game.

Unauthorised Paradox is a multiplayer horror game developed for the Meta Horizon Store.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Age and Audience

Unauthorised Paradox is intended for a broad audience, including children, teenagers, and adults.

For Meta Horizon, the Game is intended to support the Mixed Ages category, which includes users aged 10–12 and users aged 13 and older, subject to the minimum age and other requirements applicable in the user's region.

Users under the minimum age permitted by Meta Horizon are not permitted to use the Game.

Where required by Meta, the Game may use Meta's age-category functionality to receive an age category rather than the user's exact date of birth.

Because the Game is intended for Mixed Ages, we will handle age-related information and features in accordance with Meta's requirements and applicable privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you use the Game and which features you use.

Player and Account Information

The Game uses Microsoft PlayFab for backend services and may collect or store:

  • PlayFab player/account identifiers
  • Your Meta username or display name, which may be used as your in-game name
  • Game progress
  • Cosmetics
  • Purchased bundles
  • Virtual currency
  • Purchased digital entitlements
  • Moderator or staff status
  • Ban and moderation information
  • Information required to authenticate and maintain your player account

We do not intentionally collect your email address through the Game.

Technical Information

The Game and the services it uses may process technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • Game version
  • Network and connection information
  • Error and technical information

Some of this information may be processed by third-party services used to operate the Game.

3. Voice Chat and Microphone

Unauthorised Paradox includes multiplayer voice chat.

When voice chat is enabled, the Game may access your device's microphone so that your voice can be transmitted live to other players.

Voice communications are not intentionally recorded, saved as audio files, or permanently stored by Unauthorised Paradox.

The Game may temporarily process microphone audio in memory as required for live voice communication or moderation functionality.

The Game does not intentionally use voice communications for advertising or sell voice recordings to third parties.

4. Voice Moderation and Player Reports

Unauthorised Paradox includes moderation systems designed to help enforce the Game's rules.

Players may report other players for rule violations. When a player makes a report, the Game may send moderation-related text and account information to PlayFab.

This information may include:

  • Reported player's PlayFab/player ID
  • Reported player's in-game username
  • Room or lobby code
  • Reason for the report
  • Other information reasonably necessary to investigate or enforce the Game's rules

Moderation information may be stored in PlayFab so that reports, bans, and other enforcement actions can be managed.

The report system does not upload the player's voice recording or create an audio file of the voice chat.

A temporary mute setting may also be held in the Game's memory (RAM) while the mute is active or while the Game is running.

5. Multiplayer and Networking

The Game uses Mirror Networking for multiplayer functionality.

Information necessary to operate multiplayer sessions may be transmitted between players and/or the Game's networking infrastructure.

This may include player identifiers, network information, gameplay state, and information necessary to synchronize players within a multiplayer session.

Mirror is used for multiplayer networking and is not intended to serve as the Game's main player-account database.

6. Purchases and Virtual Items

Unauthorised Paradox offers optional in-app purchases.

Depending on the products available, users may purchase:

  • Cosmetics
  • Cosmetic bundles
  • Virtual currency
  • Other digital items or features, including moderator/mod-menu features

Some products are intended to be durable purchases, meaning the entitlement can remain associated with a player's account after the Game is uninstalled and later reinstalled.

Virtual currency may be purchased multiple times.

Other products, such as certain bundles or moderator features, may be limited to a single purchase.

Payment Processing

Purchases made through the Meta Horizon Store will use Meta's Platform In-App Purchase system.

We do not intentionally collect or store your complete credit-card number, debit-card number, bank-account information, or other full payment credentials.

We may receive information necessary to verify and provide a purchase, such as:

  • Product identifier
  • Transaction or purchase identifier
  • Purchase status
  • Player/account identifier
  • Digital entitlement information

7. PlayFab and Saved Purchases

The Game uses PlayFab to store player-related information and digital entitlements.

This may include:

  • Player progress
  • Cosmetics
  • Bundles
  • Virtual currency balances
  • Purchase-related information
  • Moderator status
  • Moderation and ban information

This means that uninstalling the Game does not necessarily delete server-side player information.

If you reinstall the Game and sign in using the same supported account, certain progress or purchased entitlements may be restored.

8. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your Game account.

To request deletion, contact:

info.kinetixstudios@gmail.com

Your request should include enough information for us to identify the relevant Game account while avoiding unnecessary personal information.

After verifying the request, we will process the deletion request in accordance with applicable law, Meta's platform requirements, and the capabilities of the services we use.

Where appropriate, this may include deleting the associated PlayFab player account and associated PlayFab data.

Deleting an account may permanently remove:

  • Game progress
  • Cosmetics
  • Virtual currency
  • Purchased or earned Game items
  • Moderator features
  • Other account-linked information

Purchases or transaction records independently retained by Meta or another payment provider may not be deleted from those companies' systems solely because Game data is deleted from our systems.

We may retain limited information where required or permitted by law, such as information necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, financial or transaction records, legal obligations, or enforcement of legal rights.

9. Access and Correction

You may contact us at info.kinetixstudios@gmail.com to request access to personal information we hold about you or to request correction of inaccurate information.

We may need to verify the relevant Game account before processing a request.

10. Children's Privacy

Because the Game is intended for Mixed Ages, children may use the Game.

The Game is designed to avoid intentionally requesting unnecessary personal information from children.

We do not intentionally request a child's:

  • Home address
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Payment-card number
  • Exact date of birth

The Game may nevertheless process account, device, network, gameplay, moderation, and purchase-related information necessary to operate the Game.

11. Meta Age Information

Where the Game uses Meta's age-category functionality, Meta may provide the Game with an age category associated with the current user.

We use this information only for purposes permitted by Meta's policies and applicable law, such as determining age-appropriate handling of the Game experience.

We do not intentionally attempt to determine a user's exact age from their voice, appearance, gameplay, or other unrelated information.

12. Advertising

We do not sell or share player information for targeted advertising purposes.
